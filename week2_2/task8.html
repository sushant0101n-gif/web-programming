How Online Gaming May Be Making the New Generation More Introverted

September 14, 2026

Background

Online gaming has become one of the most popular forms of entertainment among the younger generation. With fast internet connections and powerful devices, people can play games with friends and strangers without leaving their homes. Many games also use VoIP communication, allowing players to talk while playing.

However, spending many hours playing games can reduce the amount of time some young people spend outside their rooms. Instead of meeting friends face-to-face, they may communicate through a headset or chat box. Simple commands such as /join can connect players to online communities within seconds.

Technology can bring people together, but the way we use it can also affect how we interact with people in the real world.

Gachon Tech News Editorial Team

Analysis

Online gaming is not necessarily a bad thing. Games can help people make friends, improve teamwork, and develop problem-solving skills. However, when gaming replaces most face-to-face activities, it may become harder for some young people to maintain regular social interactions.

The main concern is not gaming itself but the amount of time spent on it. Spending too much time indoors and rarely interacting with people in person may contribute to a more isolated lifestyle. Finding a balance between online entertainment and outdoor or social activities could help young people enjoy gaming without becoming disconnected from the real world.

Key Facts

  • Online gaming allows people to socialize without meeting in person.
  • Many young people spend several hours a day playing games.
  • Gaming can provide teamwork and communication opportunities.
  • Too much gaming may reduce time spent on outdoor and face-to-face activities.
