Egg Fried Rice

What You Need

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 1/2 cup chopped vegetables
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 spring onion, chopped
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Heat the oil in a pan over medium heat.
  2. Beat the eggs in a bowl and pour them into the pan.
  3. Scramble the eggs gently until they are just cooked.
  4. Add the vegetables and stir for 1 to 2 minutes.
  5. Put the cooked rice into the pan and mix well.
  6. Add the soy sauce, salt, and pepper, then stir until everything is evenly coated.
  7. Do not stir too much, or the rice may become mushy.
  8. Sprinkle the spring onion on top and serve while it is still warm.
  9. Enjoy your egg fried rice with extra chili sauce if you like.
